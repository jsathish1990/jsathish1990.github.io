Data Science and ML/AI Engineer with six years of professional experience in data analytics, machine learning, and deep learning. Proficient in Python and PyTorch, with hands-on experience building NLP pipelines using large language models (GPT-3.5/4), LangChain, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Most recently at **Eviden**, I worked on LLM fine-tuning, question-answering over documents, voice-to-text integration (OpenAI Whisper, NVIDIA NeMo), and LLM safety with GuardrailsAI. I also develop and teach courses on **PySpark** and **Generative AI** on Udemy.

My background spans both industry and academia — including graduate research at **Georgia Institute of Technology** on time-series analytics and data-driven modeling in power systems.

I enjoy working on complex problems at the intersection of AI and real-world systems, and I am actively looking for opportunities where I can apply and grow my expertise in applied ML and AI engineering.
